# PTB-XL attribution

The recordings in this folder are a curated subset of the PTB-XL dataset, redistributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).

Wagner P, Strodthoff N, Bousseljot R, Samek W, Schaeffter T. PTB-XL, a large publicly available electrocardiography dataset (v1.0.3). PhysioNet, 2022. https://doi.org/10.13026/kfzx-aw45

Goldberger A, Amaral L, Glass L, et al. PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and PhysioNet: Components of a new research resource for complex physiologic signals. Circulation. 2000;101(23):e215–e220.

Signals are stored as little-endian int16 microvolts (base64) at 500 Hz, unchanged from the source apart from unit conversion.
